## Veeramah Lab Stuff
This is my (An's) repo for all of the lab things I do in Dr. Krishna Veeramah's lab. 
It contains primarily scripts I use for things I need to do. Many are pulled from Krishna and/or Deven Vyas, and are credited if so.
I also wrote out some tutorials for processes I found to be confusing when I initially was carrying them out. They may not be the most comprehensive, but may be useful for someone else who had no idea what was going on! 

Below is sort of a key for my various folders/projects:
- `histogenes_slovenia`: Files I need for analyzing aDNA X chromosome data from the Histogenes project. Many scripts pulled from Deven Vyas.
- `mouse_lemur`: Files related to any of my mouse lemur projects.
    - `ml_pop`: Specifically for my population genetics-focused projects.
- `misc`: Anything else.
